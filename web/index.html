Browser-based full-wave FDTD. Simulate the built-in grounded CPW line or load a board (KiCad, or Gerber + drill with the stackup entered below) (a net that runs from the left to the right board edge, over a solid bottom plane). Mode ports launch the line's own eigenmode; results show S in 50 Ω or normalised to the line Z0. Solver: double-precision WebAssembly or single-precision WebGPU.
